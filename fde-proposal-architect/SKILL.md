## Purpose

Create compelling, credible proposals for Forward Deployed Engineer engagements. Not generic consulting proposals — embedded, accountable, system-shipping proposals that convert.

**Trigger:** A prospect has completed the AI Sales Process Audit or reached out directly. Time to propose the engagement.

---

## The FDE Proposal Framework

### Principle: The Proposal IS the Product

> "Your proposal should be so clear, so specific, and so aligned with their pain that signing it feels like the obvious next step."

---

## Section 1: The Hook (1 Page)

### 1.1 Restate Their Problem

**Template:**
```
[Prospect Company] is losing [X hours/week] to [specific admin tasks].
This costs approximately €[X/year] in lost selling time.

Your current workflow:
- [Specific pain point 1 from audit]
- [Specific pain point 2 from audit]
- [Specific pain point 3 from audit]

The result: [quantified impact]
```

**Rule:** Use their exact words from the audit. No generic language.

### 1.2 The Transformation

**Template:**
```
After our engagement:
- [Specific outcome 1] → saves [X hours/week]
- [Specific outcome 2] → reduces [specific friction]
- [Specific outcome 3] → increases [specific metric]

Your team will: [what they'll do differently]
You will: [what you'll see differently]
```

```

Week 1-2: Discovery + System Design
- I sit with your team, map your workflow

- 30-day support period
- Performance monitoring setup

After: Optional retainer (€1,500/mo)
- System optimization

- Team training updates
```

## Section 4: Credibility (1 Page)

### 4.1 Relevant Experience

**Template:**
```
Similar engagements:
- [Industry] company, [size], [specific result]
- [Industry] company, [size], [specific result]

(Note: In early stages, be honest: "This is my first FDE engagement
of this type. Here's why I'm confident:")
```

**Authenticity rule:** If no similar engagements, say so. Then explain:
- 15 years in B2B sales (you know the pain)
- Built systems for your own business (you ship)
- Audit process de-risks the engagement (they see value before committing)

### 4.2 Risk Reversal

**The FDE Guarantee:**
```
If after Week 2 you don't believe the system will work,
you can pause the engagement. No questions asked.

You'll keep the audit report and system design.
You pay only for work completed to date.
```

## Proposal Checklist

Before sending, verify:

- [ ] Every claim matches audit findings
- [ ] All numbers use € currency
- [ ] No generic language — their words, their pain
- [ ] Deliverables are specific and measurable
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (not optimistic)
- [ ] Pricing is clear, no hidden fees
- [ ] Risk reversal is genuine
- [ ] CTA is simple (one action)

## Anti-Patterns

### Anti-Pattern 1: Generic Proposal
**Symptom:** Could be sent to any company. Uses filler language.
**Fix:** Use their exact words from the audit. Name their specific tools, their team size, their pain.

### Anti-Pattern 2: Feature Dump
**Symptom:** Lists every possible deliverable. Overwhelms.
**Fix:** 3-5 specific outcomes max. Each tied to their pain.

### Anti-Pattern 3: Over-Promising
**Symptom:** "10x your revenue", "transform your business"
**Fix:** Quantify conservatively. Under-promise, over-deliver.

### Anti-Pattern 4: No Risk Reversal
**Symptom:** "Pay me €5,500 and hope it works"
**Fix:** The FDE Guarantee. Pause anytime. Keep the work product.
