# Leak-to-Retainer — Self-Evaluation Tests

## Test 1: Signal Detection

**Scenario:** Plumbing company in Austin, 12 Google reviews.

**Check:** Can the agent identify high-signal reviews and score the lead?

**Expected:**
- 3+ reviews mention phone/call issues
- Lead score: 14+ out of 20
- Signal strength: 🔴 High

**Pass criteria:** Specific reviews cited, score calculated, priority confirmed

---

## Test 2: Outreach Personalization

**Scenario:** Building a 3-touch sequence for the plumbing company.

**Check:** Does every touch use the prospect's own words?

**Expected:**
- Touch 1: References specific review verbatim
- Touch 2: Voicemail references same review
- Touch 3: Case study relevant to plumbing/HVAC
- No mention of "AI" or "automation" in any touch

**Pass criteria:** All 3 touches reference verifiable signals, under 150 words each

---

## Test 3: Call Brief Completeness

**Scenario:** Preparing for a sales call.

**Check:** Does the brief include all required sections?

**Expected:**
- Prospect name + industry
- 3+ specific signals
- Leak estimate with math
- Competitor gap
- 3+ talking points
- 3+ objection preps
- Clear ask

**Pass criteria:** All sections present, one page max

---

## Test 4: Proposal Structure

**Scenario:** Sending a one-page proposal.

**Check:** Does the proposal follow the 5-section structure?

**Expected:**
- THE LEAK: Uses prospect's exact words
- THE FIX: Specific capabilities, ~14 days
- THE PILOT: 30-day cancel-anytime
- THE INVESTMENT: Setup + monthly
- EXPECTED ROI: Calculated from their data
- NEXT STEP: Simple CTA

**Pass criteria:** All sections present, pricing appropriate for tier, one page max

---

## Test 5: ROI Report Lead Number

**Scenario:** Monthly report for an active client.

**Check:** Does the report lead with revenue protected?

**Expected:**
- Biggest number first
- Specific story with time + value
- Comparison to old solution
- Upsell suggestion at end

**Pass criteria:** Revenue protected is the headline, specific story included, upsell present
