# Leak-to-Retainer — Signal Detection

## Sources to Monitor

| Source | What to Look For | Signal Strength |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Google Reviews | Complaints about "never answer," "voicemail full," "called 3 times" | 🔴 High |
| Facebook/Nextdoor | Posts asking for recommendations; complaints about unresponsive businesses | 🔴 High |
| Website audit | No online booking, no chat widget, phone number only, old contact form | 🟡 Medium |
| PPC spend | Running Google Ads but no call tracking or intake system | 🟡 Medium |
| Industry forums | HVAC/plumbing/electrical groups mentioning seasonal overflow | 🟡 Medium |
| Job postings | Hiring receptionists (signals they know they have a problem) | 🟢 Low |

## Lead Scoring Matrix

Score each lead 1–5 on:
- **Leak severity**: How obvious and costly is the problem? (1 = minor, 5 = revenue hemorrhaging)
- **Revenue potential**: What's their typical job value? (1 = < $500, 5 = > $5,000)
- **Readiness**: How urgent is their need? (1 = exploring, 5 = actively hiring/help-seeking)
- **Access**: Can you reach the decision-maker? (1 = gatekeeper-only, 5 = owner-operator)

**Prioritize leads scoring 14+ out of 20.**
