# Leak-to-Retainer — Call Brief Template

Generate a one-page brief before every sales call:

```
PROSPECT: [Business Name] | [Owner Name]
INDUSTRY: [e.g., HVAC, Plumbing, Roofing]

SIGNALS:
  • [Specific review mentioning phone issue]
  • [Website gap: no online booking]
  • [Ad spend visible: $X/month, no call tracking]

LEAK ESTIMATE: $[X]/month in missed calls × $[Y] avg job value

COMPETITOR GAP: [Top competitor has 24/7 chat, they don't]

TALKING POINTS:
  • "I saw a review saying..." (use their words)
  • "Companies like yours typically miss [X]% of after-hours calls"
  • "One [industry] client in [nearby city] added $[Z]/month in booked jobs"

OBJECTION PREP:
  • "We're fine, we have a receptionist" → "What happens at 6 PM? Weekends?"
  • "Too expensive" → "What's the cost of one missed $[Y] job?"
  • "We tried a service before" → "What broke? Here's how we're different..."

ASK: 30-day pilot, $[setup] + $[1,500–3,000]/month
```
