# Leak-to-Retainer Sales System

A skill for systematically identifying, approaching, and closing local service businesses that are leaking revenue due to poor call handling, lack of after-hours coverage, or missing intake systems.

## What This Skill Enables

1. **Signal Detection** — Find businesses with visible leakage (missed calls, bad reviews mentioning phone issues, no online booking, slow response times)
2. **Prioritization** — Rank leads by leakage severity, revenue potential, and readiness to buy
3. **Outreach Sequencing** — Multi-touch campaigns (email, voicemail, LinkedIn) that lead with value, not pitch
4. **Call Preparation** — One-page briefs with the prospect's specific leaks, competitor gaps, and tailored talking points
5. **Proposal Generation** — One-page proposals: their leak in their own words, the fix, pilot terms, pricing
6. **ROI Reporting** — Monthly one-page reports showing calls caught, appointments booked, revenue protected

---

## Step 1: Signal Detection — Find the Leaks

### Sources to Monitor

| Source | What to Look For | Signal Strength |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Google Reviews | Complaints about "never answer," "voicemail full," "called 3 times" | 🔴 High |
| Facebook/Nextdoor | Posts asking for recommendations; complaints about unresponsive businesses | 🔴 High |
| Website audit | No online booking, no chat widget, phone number only, old contact form | 🟡 Medium |
| PPC spend | Running Google Ads but no call tracking or intake system | 🟡 Medium |
| Industry forums | HVAC/plumbing/electrical groups mentioning seasonal overflow | 🟡 Medium |
| Job postings | Hiring receptionists (signals they know they have a problem) | 🟢 Low |

### Lead Scoring Matrix

Score each lead 1–5 on:
- **Leak severity**: How obvious and costly is the problem? (1 = minor, 5 = revenue hemorrhaging)
- **Revenue potential**: What's their typical job value? (1 = < $500, 5 = > $5,000)
- **Readiness**: How urgent is their need? (1 = exploring, 5 = actively hiring/help-seeking)
- **Access**: Can you reach the decision-maker? (1 = gatekeeper-only, 5 = owner-operator)

**Prioritize leads scoring 14+ out of 20.**

## Step 2: Outreach Sequence — Lead With Value

### 3-Touch Sequence (7-Day Cycle)

**Touch 1 — Value-First Email (Day 1)**
- Subject: "[City] [Industry] — I found something on your Google reviews"
- Body: Reference a specific review mentioning phone/call issues. No pitch. Offer a free 2-minute audit of their call handling. Close with a specific time option.

**Touch 2 — Voicemail Drop (Day 3)**
- 30-second voicemail referencing the same review. Mention the audit. Leave a callback number.

**Touch 3 — LinkedIn/Email Follow-Up (Day 7)**
- Share a relevant case study or stat (e.g., "HVAC companies lose 35% of after-hours calls to competitors"). Offer the audit again. Include a Calendly link.

### Rules
- Never lead with "AI" or "automation" — lead with "missed revenue" or "customer frustration"
- Every touch must reference a specific, verifiable signal (review, website gap, ad spend)
- Keep all outreach under 150 words
- Use the prospect's own language from reviews/posts

## Step 3: Call Brief — Pre-Call Intelligence

Generate a one-page brief before every sales call:

```
PROSPECT: [Business Name] | [Owner Name]
INDUSTRY: [e.g., HVAC, Plumbing, Roofing]
SIGNALS:
  • [Specific review mentioning phone issue]
  • [Website gap: no online booking]
  • [Ad spend visible: $X/month, no call tracking]
LEAK ESTIMATE: $[X]/month in missed calls × $[Y] avg job value
COMPETITOR GAP: [Top competitor has 24/7 chat, they don't]
TALKING POINTS:
  • "I saw a review saying..." (use their words)
  • "Companies like yours typically miss [X]% of after-hours calls"
  • "One [industry] client in [nearby city] added $[Z]/month in booked jobs"
OBJECTION PREP:
  • "We're fine, we have a receptionist" → "What happens at 6 PM? Weekends?"
  • "Too expensive" → "What's the cost of one missed $[Y] job?"
  • "We tried a service before" → "What broke? Here's how we're different..."
ASK: 30-day pilot, $[setup] + $[1,500–3,000]/month
```

## Step 4: One-Page Proposal — The Close

### Structure (90-Second Read)

```
THE LEAK (Their Words)
"[Direct quote from their review or what they said on the call]"
→ [Your calculation: X missed calls/month × $Y job value = $Z lost]

THE FIX
Pre-built AI reception system installed by a contractor in ~14 days:
• Answers every call 24/7 — no voicemail, no hold
• Books appointments directly into [their calendar system]
• Qualifies leads and routes emergencies instantly
• Speaks English/Spanish (or: matches their customer base)

THE PILOT
30-day cancel-anytime pilot. No long-term contract.
If it doesn't pay for itself in the first month, cancel free.

THE INVESTMENT
Setup: $[X]
Monthly retainer: $[1,500–3,000] (scales with call volume)

EXPECTED ROI
Based on [their call volume/lead value]:
• Calls captured: [X]/month
• Appointments booked: [Y]/month  
• Revenue protected: $[Z]/month
• ROI in Month 1: [X]x

NEXT STEP
Reply YES to this email or call [number] to start the pilot.
```

### Pricing Guidance

| Tier | Monthly Retainer | Best For |
|------|-----------------|----------|
| Starter | $1,500 | Solo operators, < 50 calls/week |
| Growth | $2,250 | 2–5 techs, 50–150 calls/week |
| Scale | $3,000 | 6+ techs, 150+ calls/week, 24/7 needs |

Setup fee: $500–$2,000 depending on complexity (calendar integrations, CRM, custom intake flows).

- Send by email + SMS to owner

## Key Principles

1. **Their words, not yours** — Every proposal and call brief must use the prospect's own language from reviews, calls, or social posts.
2. **Pilot first, contract later** — 30-day cancel-anything removes risk and accelerates decision-making.
3. **Show, don't tell** — ROI reports with real numbers and specific stories beat generic promises.
4. **Price on value, not hours** — Retainer scales with their revenue potential, not your time spent.
5. **One page, every time** — Proposals, briefs, and reports must fit on one page. Busy owners don't read decks.

## Example Workflow

**Input:** "I found a plumbing company in Austin with 12 Google reviews complaining about missed calls. Help me build the pipeline."

**Output:**
1. **Signal log** — 12 reviews documented, 3 mention after-hours specifically
2. **Lead score** — 17/20 (high leak, high job value $800–$2,500, owner-operator, actively hiring)
3. **Outreach sequence** — 3-touch email/voicemail/LinkedIn using their exact review language
4. **Call brief** — One-pager with leak estimate ($18K–$45K/month lost), competitor gap, objections
5. **Proposal** — One-pager: their words, 14-day install, 30-day pilot, $1,500 setup + $2,250/month
6. **Monthly report** — Calls caught, appointments booked, revenue protected vs. old receptionist cost
