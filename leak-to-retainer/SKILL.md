# Leak-to-Retainer Sales System

A skill for systematically identifying, approaching, and closing local service businesses that are leaking revenue due to poor call handling, lack of after-hours coverage, or missing intake systems.

## What This Skill Enables

1. **Signal Detection** — Find businesses with visible leakage
2. **Prioritization** — Rank leads by leakage severity, revenue potential, readiness
3. **Outreach Sequencing** — Multi-touch campaigns leading with value
4. **Call Preparation** — One-page briefs with prospect-specific intelligence
5. **Proposal Generation** — One-page proposals using their own words
6. **ROI Reporting** — Monthly one-page reports showing revenue protected

---

```
[Signal Detection] → [Lead Scoring] → [Outreach Sequence]
                                           ↓
[Monthly ROI] ← [Retainer] ← [Proposal] ← [Call Brief]
```

**5 steps:** See references/workflow-steps.md for full detail on each step.

---

## Key Principles

1. **Their words, not yours** — Every proposal and call brief must use the prospect's own language
2. **Pilot first, contract later** — 30-day cancel-anything removes risk
3. **Show, don't tell** — ROI reports with real numbers beat generic promises
4. **Price on value, not hours** — Retainer scales with revenue potential
5. **One page, every time** — Proposals, briefs, reports must fit on one page

## Example Workflow

**Input:** "I found a plumbing company in Austin with 12 Google reviews complaining about missed calls."

**Output:**
1. Signal log — 12 reviews documented
2. Lead score — 17/20
3. Outreach sequence — 3-touch using their exact review language
4. Call brief — One-pager with leak estimate ($18K–$45K/month lost)
5. Proposal — One-pager, 14-day install, 30-day pilot, $1,500 setup + $2,250/month
6. Monthly report — Calls caught, appointments booked, revenue protected
