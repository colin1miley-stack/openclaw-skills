# Prospect Intelligence Framework

A methodology for auditing a business's digital footprint and identifying revenue optimization opportunities.

## Overview

This framework describes how to systematically evaluate a prospect's website, social media presence, and operational infrastructure to identify gaps that may be causing revenue leakage.

## The 20-Point Signal Checklist

### Contact & Conversion
- [ ] Contact form present and functional
- [ ] Phone number visible and clickable
- [ ] Email link or contact method
- [ ] Booking/scheduling system (Calendly, etc.)
- [ ] Live chat or chatbot
- [ ] WhatsApp Business integration

### E-commerce & Monetization
- [ ] Online shop or booking system
- [ ] Pricing page or packages visible
- [ ] Abandoned cart recovery
- [ ] Payment gateway integration

### Marketing & Content
- [ ] Blog or content hub
- [ ] Newsletter signup
- [ ] Testimonials or reviews
- [ ] Case studies or portfolio
- [ ] Video content

### Analytics & Tracking
- [ ] Google Analytics or equivalent
- [ ] Conversion tracking
- [ ] Facebook Pixel or ad tracking
- [ ] Cookie compliance / GDPR

### Social Presence
- [ ] Instagram
- [ ] LinkedIn
- [ ] TikTok
- [ ] Facebook
- [ ] YouTube
- [ ] Pinterest

## The Audit Process

### Step 1: Website Scraping
Extract business information, contact methods, tech stack indicators, and content signals from the prospect's website.

### Step 2: Social Discovery
Find and validate social media presence across major platforms. Look for active engagement, follower counts, and content strategy.

### Step 3: Gap Analysis
Score each checkpoint. Identify which signals are missing and what that implies about the business's operational maturity.

### Step 4: Revenue Impact Estimation
For each missing signal, estimate the potential revenue impact based on:
- Industry benchmarks
- Business size/revenue
- Competitor analysis
- Seasonal factors

### Step 5: Service Alignment
Map identified gaps to specific service offerings. Prioritize by:
- Highest revenue impact
- Fastest implementation
- Lowest friction for the client

## Output Format

A professional audit report should include:

1. **Executive Summary** — High-level findings and recommendation
2. **Business Profile** — Key facts about the prospect
3. **Digital Footprint Audit** — Checklist results with status
4. **Revenue Leak Assessment** — Quantified gaps with €/£/$ estimates
5. **Service Alignment Matrix** — Which services address which gaps
6. **Recommended Implementation Plan** — Phased approach with priorities
7. **ROI Projections** — Expected return on investment

## Revenue Scaling Formula

To estimate revenue impact for a specific business:

```
Estimated Leakage = Annual Revenue × Leak Factor × Industry Adjustment

Where:
- Leak Factor: 0.05 to 0.25 (based on gap severity)
- Industry Adjustment: 0.8 to 1.2 (based on industry benchmarks)
```

**Example:**
- Business: Wedding florist
- Annual Revenue: €250,000
- Primary Gap: No instant response to enquiries
- Leak Factor: 0.15 (15% of enquiries lost)
- Industry Adjustment: 1.1 (wedding industry = higher urgency)
- Estimated Leakage: €250,000 × 0.15 × 1.1 = **€41,250/year**

## Implementation Priority Matrix

| Priority | Criteria | Typical Services |
|----------|----------|-----------------|
| **P0** | Revenue capture (existing demand) | CRM, auto-responder, booking |
| **P1** | Leak prevention (stop losing) | Chatbot, nurture sequences |
| **P2** | Demand generation (new leads) | Content, SEO, social |
| **P3** | Optimization (improve existing) | A/B testing, analytics |

## The Proposal Structure

A winning audit proposal should:

1. **Lead with the leak** — "You're losing €X/year to [specific gap]"
2. **Show the math** — Revenue × gap × industry factor = specific number
3. **Map to solution** — Each gap → specific service → specific outcome
4. **Phase the work** — Quick wins first, long-term builds second
5. **Price the ROI** — "Spend €X to recover €Y = Z% ROI in N weeks"

## Usage

This framework is designed to be adapted to any service business. Customize the:
- Service matrix (your specific offerings)
- Pricing (your rates)
- Entry angles (your sales approach)
- Industry benchmarks (your market data)
