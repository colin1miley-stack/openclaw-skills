## Scoring

- **High weight:** 3 points
- **Medium weight:** 2 points
- **Low weight:** 1 point

**Maximum score:** 50 points

**Grading:**
- 40-50: Excellent digital presence
- 30-39: Good, some gaps
- 20-29: Moderate, significant gaps
- 10-19: Poor, major leaks
- 0-9: Critical, immediate action needed
