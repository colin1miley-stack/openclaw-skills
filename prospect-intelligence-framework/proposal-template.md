# Proposal Template

---

### Page 1: Cover
- Your company logo/branding
- Prospect company name
- Date
- "Revenue Leak Audit — [Prospect Name]"

---

### Page 2: Executive Summary

**One paragraph:**
> [Prospect Name] is a [industry] business with strong [strengths] but significant revenue leakage due to [primary gaps]. This audit identifies €X–€Y in annual leaks and recommends a phased fix.

**Key numbers (3 bullets):**
- Annual Revenue: €[amount]
- Estimated Leakage: €[range]
- Recommended Investment: €[amount]
- Expected ROI: [X]% in [timeframe]

## Design Tips

- Use your brand colors and fonts
- Include prospect's logo (if available)
- One graph/chart per page maximum
- Lead with numbers, follow with explanation
- End every page with a clear takeaway
