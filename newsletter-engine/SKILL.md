## Purpose

A systematic approach to creating, publishing, and growing a newsletter for AI Revenue Systems. Not just "write when you feel like it" — a production system that compounds audience and authority over time.

**Newsletter Name:** *"Skills, Not Tools"*  
**Tagline:** *What actually works in AI revenue systems.*  
**Frequency:** Weekly (Monday mornings)  
**Platform:** ConvertKit (when ready) / Substack (interim)

## 3. Issue Structure Template

### Standard Issue Format (800–1,200 words)

```
SUBJECT LINE: [Specific outcome] in [timeframe] — here's how

PREVIEW TEXT: Not the framework. The messy reality.

---

OPENING (100 words)
- Hook: Specific, surprising, or contrarian
- Context: Why this matters now
- Promise: What you'll learn

THE STORY (400 words)
- What happened (specific, real)
- What went wrong (honest)
- What worked (the insight)
- Numbers (quantified where possible)

THE SYSTEM (300 words)
- The framework/step-by-step
- How to apply it
- Common mistakes to avoid

THE CTA (100 words)
- What to do next
- Link to: audit booking, related post, tool

---

P.S. [Personal note, question, or behind-the-scenes detail]
```

## 4. Subject Line Formulas

| Formula | Example | Why It Works |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Specific outcome + timeframe** | "€2,150 in 48 hours — the audit that changed everything" | Concrete, curiosity gap |
| **Contrarian take** | "Why most AI 'strategies' fail (and what to do instead)" | Challenges assumptions |
| **Behind the scenes** | "I almost quit last week" | Vulnerability, relatability |
| **How I [action]** | "How I built a 24/7 lead intake system in 3 days" | Process, learnable |
| **The [number] things** | "The 3 automations that actually matter" | Scannable, specific |
| **Question** | "Is your CRM leaking revenue?" | Direct, problem-aware |

**Rule:** Write 10 subject lines. Pick the one that makes YOU want to open.

## 5. Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Do
- Write like you talk (sharp, practical, no corporate speak)
- Share numbers (even when they're not impressive)
- Admit mistakes ("I tried X. It didn't work. Here's why.")
- Use "I" and "you" (personal, direct)
- Include one specific, actionable takeaway

### Don't
- Use jargon without explaining it
- Write long paragraphs (max 3 sentences)
- Sound like a LinkedIn influencer ("Grateful to announce...")
- Over-promise ("This will 10x your...")
- Forget the CTA (every issue needs one clear next step)

## 6. Growth Strategy

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–3)
- **Goal:** 100 subscribers
- **Tactics:**
  - LinkedIn CTA in every post ("Subscribe for weekly systems")
  - Lead magnet: "5-Minute Sales Audit" PDF
  - Website email capture (EmailCapture.tsx)
  - Personal outreach to 20 sales directors

### Phase 2: Amplification (Months 4–6)
- **Goal:** 500 subscribers
- **Tactics:**
  - Guest posts on sales/AI newsletters
  - Podcast appearances (sales podcasts)
  - LinkedIn document posts (carousels from newsletter content)
  - Referral program ("Share with 3 friends, get the exclusive playbook")

### Phase 3: Monetization (Months 7–12)
- **Goal:** 1,000+ subscribers, first sponsorship
- **Tactics:**
  - Sponsored placements (AI tools, sales software)
  - Paid newsletter tier (deeper dives, templates, community)
  - Course launch ("The FDE System")

## 10. Anti-Patterns

### Anti-Pattern 1: "When I Feel Like It"
**Symptom:** Inconsistent publishing, bursts then silence.
**Fix:** Schedule issues in advance. Batch 4 weeks at a time.

### Anti-Pattern 2: Listicle Overload
**Symptom:** Every issue is "5 ways to..." "10 tools that..."
**Fix:** One deep story beats five shallow tips.

### Anti-Pattern 3: No CTA
**Symptom:** Great content, no next step. Missed opportunity.
**Fix:** Every issue has ONE clear CTA. Not three. One.

### Anti-Pattern 4: Selling Too Early
**Symptom:** First 5 issues are pitches. Unsubscribes spike.
**Fix:** 80% value, 20% pitch. Earn the right to sell.
