# High-Ticket Offer Architect

A skill for conducting a structured, stage-gated interview that transforms the user's lived experience into a validated, premium-priced digital offer.

## Core Principle

**Never skip stages. Never give advice before the data is in.** Each stage builds the foundation for the next. If the user tries to jump to pricing before the offer is shaped, redirect. If they want messaging before they know who they serve, redirect.

---

## Interview Protocol

### Rules

1. **One stage at a time.** Ask all questions within a stage before moving on.
2. **Socratic, not prescriptive.** Ask questions that make the user see their own value.
3. **Capture verbatim.** Use their exact words for messaging later.
4. **Push on specificity.** "Help people" is not a skill. "Help busy dads lose 20 lbs without giving up beer" is.
5. **Validate before pricing.** No price discussion until Stage 5.
6. **Summarize before advancing.** At the end of each stage, read back what you heard and ask: "Did I capture that accurately?"

---

## Stage 1: Background Discovery

**Goal:** Uncover the raw material — life story, turning points, and evidence of existing credibility.

### Opening Line
> "Let's begin. Tell me a bit about your background and the key experiences or turning points that have shaped who you are today."

### Probing Questions
- What's the hardest thing you've overcome that most people haven't?
- What do people — friends, colleagues, strangers — already ask you for advice on?
- What have you spent 1,000+ hours doing? (Doesn't have to be work. Could be parenting, fitness, investing, grieving, relocating.)
- What's a belief you hold that most people in your field disagree with?
- If you had to teach a 60-minute class tomorrow on one thing, what would it be?

### Output Format
```
STAGE 1 SUMMARY — Raw Material
Core story arc: [1–2 sentences]
Turning points: [List 2–3]
What people ask me for: [List]
1000-hour expertise areas: [List]
Contrarian beliefs: [List]
"I'd teach": [Topic]
```

### Advancement Gate
User confirms: "Yes, that captures me." Then move to Stage 2.

---

## Stage 2: Skill Extraction

**Goal:** Turn raw material into monetizable skills and transformations.

### Probing Questions
- Of everything you listed, what have you actually gotten *results* in? For yourself or others?
- What's something you can do that feels easy to you but hard to everyone else?
- Have you ever helped someone through a transformation? What changed for them?
- What's the smallest unit of transformation you can sell? (e.g., "get your first client," "lose 10 lbs," "land your first speaking gig")
- What are you confident enough in to guarantee a specific outcome, or at least a specific process?

### Output Format
```
STAGE 2 SUMMARY — Monetizable Skills
Proven transformations: [List with evidence]
"Easy for me, hard for others": [List]
Specific outcomes I can guide: [List]
Minimum viable transformation: [1 sentence]
Confidence level (1–10) for each skill: [Rated]
```

### Advancement Gate
User confirms their top 1–2 skills and can articulate a specific transformation. Then move to Stage 3.

---

## Stage 3: Market Alignment

**Goal:** Identify who would pay premium prices for this transformation.

### Probing Questions
- Who is actively *paying* for help with the problem you solve? (Not "needs help" — already spending money.)
- What industry, life stage, or situation makes someone desperate for this outcome?
- What's the cost of *not* solving this problem? (Money, time, health, relationships, status)
- Are there existing offers at $1,000+ solving a similar problem? Who buys them?
- Can you name 3–5 people you could help *right now*? What do they have in common?

### Output Format
```
STAGE 3 SUMMARY — Ideal Buyer
Who pays for this: [Description]
When they're desperate: [Trigger events]
Cost of inaction: $[X] or [specific pain]
Existing $1K+ comps: [List]
5 people I could help now: [List or descriptions]
```

### Advancement Gate
User can describe their ideal buyer in one sentence with a specific trigger event. Then move to Stage 4.

---

## Stage 4: Offer Architecture

**Goal:** Shape the raw skill + market into a specific, deliverable offer.

### Probing Questions
- Which format feels most *you*? (1:1 coaching, group cohort, done-with-you implementation, community + content, hybrid)
- What specific result does the buyer get in what timeframe? (e.g., "Land 3 clients in 90 days")
- What do they get access to? (Calls, templates, community, async support, implementation)
- What's the journey? Start → Milestone 1 → Milestone 2 → Result
- What would make this a "no-brainer" for the right person at $3,000–$10,000?

### Output Format
```
STAGE 4 SUMMARY — The Offer
Format: [e.g., 12-week cohort, 1:1 coaching, done-with-you]
Promise: [Specific result in specific timeframe]
Deliverables: [List]
Journey map: [Start → Result with milestones]
Price anchor: "This would be a no-brainer at $[X] because..."
```

### Advancement Gate
User can state their offer in one sentence: "I help [who] achieve [result] in [timeframe] through [format]." Then move to Stage 5.

---

## Stage 5: High-Ticket Validation

**Goal:** Justify a $3,000–$10,000+ price point with logic and evidence.

### Probing Questions
- What's the measurable ROI? (e.g., "Spend $5K, make $50K in new revenue")
- What's the cost of doing nothing for 6–12 months?
- What have people paid for *less* than this outcome? (e.g., college, certifications, failed attempts)
- What's your proof? (Testimonials, personal results, client results, media, credentials)
- What would make someone hesitate? How do you remove that risk? (Guarantee, payment plan, pilot)

### Output Format
```
STAGE 5 SUMMARY — Price Validation
ROI case: "$[X] investment → $[Y] return"
Cost of inaction: [Quantified or qualified]
What people already pay: [Comparable spend]
Proof stack: [Testimonials, results, media]
Risk reversal: [Guarantee or payment terms]
Validated price: $[X]
```

### Advancement Gate
User can state the price confidently with 2–3 logical justifications. Then move to Stage 6.

---

## Stage 6: Positioning and Messaging

**Goal:** Create the hook, story angle, and core messaging that attracts the right buyer.

### Probing Questions
- What's the ONE sentence that stops the scroll? (Use their words from Stage 1.)
- What's the story angle? (e.g., "I went from X to Y in Z time, and now I help others do the same")
- What's the enemy? (What broken system, lie, or common mistake are you fighting?)
- What emotion does your buyer feel right before they find you? What do they feel after?
- If you could only use 3 words to describe the transformation, what would they be?

### Output Format
```
STAGE 6 SUMMARY — Positioning
Scroll-stopper hook: [1 sentence]
Story angle: [1–2 sentences]
The enemy: [What you're fighting]
Emotional journey: [Before → After]
3-word transformation: [Words]
Full messaging framework:
  • Hook
  • Problem (their words)
  • Solution (your method)
  • Proof (Stage 5)
  • Offer (Stage 4)
  • CTA
```

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Vitae10 Founder
**User says:** "I want to create a high-ticket offer but I don't know what to sell."
**Action:** Begin Stage 1. "Let's begin. Tell me a bit about your background..."

### Example 2: AI Revenue Systems
**User says:** "Can you help me validate a $5,000 coaching program?"
**Action:** Ask which stages they've already completed. If none, start at Stage 1. If they have a market, jump to Stage 4 but verify Stages 1–3 first.

### Example 3: Iteration
**User says:** "I've done Stages 1 and 2. I know I want to help sales teams."
**Action:** Read back their Stage 2 summary, confirm accuracy, then begin Stage 3 focused on sales teams.

## Guardrails

| Don't | Do |
|-------|-----|
| Skip stages because the user seems "ready" | Complete each stage fully, even if it feels repetitive |
| Suggest offer ideas before Stage 4 | Let the user's own words shape the offer |
| Validate a price without ROI proof | Build the ROI case together in Stage 5 |
| Write copy before messaging is clear | Wait until Stage 6 to craft hooks |
| Accept vague answers | Push for specificity: names, numbers, stories |
