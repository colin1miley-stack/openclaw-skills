# Writing Style — Avoid List

## Punctuation & Constructions

- **Em dashes.** Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead.
- **Semicolons.** Break into separate sentences.
- **"Not just X, but also Y."** Use "X. And Y." or "X plus Y."
- **"It's not X, it's Y."** Just say what it is.
- **Repetitive contrast formulas** such as "While X, Y."
- **Decorative punctuation** for emphasis (multiple exclamation points, etc.)
- **Excessive parenthetical explanations.** Say it or don't.

## Setup & Wrapper Language

- "In conclusion,"
- "In closing,"
- "The key takeaway is..."
- "To summarize..."
- "At the end of the day..."
- Output wrappers or meta notes ("Here is my response:", "I hope this helps:")
- Restating the user's question before answering.
- Summarizing the response at the end unless asked.

## Structural Patterns

- **Uniform paragraph length.** Mix short and long.
- **Uniform sentence length.** Vary rhythm.
- **Lists where every bullet follows the same pattern.** Break the pattern intentionally.
- **Excessive headings and subheadings.** Use them sparingly.
- **Generic headings** such as "Key Takeaways," "Why It Matters," "Benefits," or "Final Thoughts" unless requested.
- **Rhetorical questions** used as transitions.
- **Excessive transitions** between paragraphs or bullets.

## Tone & Vocabulary

- **Hedging.** ("might", "could", "perhaps", "somewhat", "arguably")
- **Unnecessary adjectives and adverbs.** Cut 50% of them.
- **Broad generalizations.**
- **Passive voice** when the actor matters.
- **Abstract classifications** and vague category language.
- **Mixing poetic language with dry technical phrasing** unless the tone calls for it.
- **Adding context the reader does not need.**

## Banned Words

These words are signals of AI-generated text:

- foster
- tapestry
- delve
- robust
- landscape
- leverage (as a verb)
- paradigm
- synergy
- holistic
- streamline
- actionable (as a noun)
- impactful (use "matters" instead)

## Repetition

- **Repeating the same idea** in the introduction, body, and ending.
- **Repeating keywords** when a natural alternative exists.
- **Forced variation** when repeating a simple term would sound more human.
