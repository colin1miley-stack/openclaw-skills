# Writing Style — Core Rules

## Language

- Use clear, simple language.
- Use short, impactful sentences.
- Use active voice.
- Focus on practical, actionable insights.
- Address the reader directly with "you" and "your".
- Prefer specific nouns, numbers, examples, and actions over abstract language.
- Sound like a person with a point of view, not a neutral summary engine.

## Sentence Craft

- Vary sentence length and structure naturally.
- Prefer short sentences. Mix in occasional longer ones for rhythm.
- Use bullet lists where helpful.
- Use specific examples to support claims when possible.
- When repeating a simple term would sound more human, don't force variation.

## Paragraph Craft

- Vary paragraph length.
- Avoid several consecutive sentences with identical structure.
- Avoid perfect structural consistency across paragraphs.
- No filler transition sentences between paragraphs.
