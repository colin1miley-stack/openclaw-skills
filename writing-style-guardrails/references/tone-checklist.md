# Writing Style — Tone Checklist

## Sound Like a Person

- [ ] Would I say this aloud in conversation?
- [ ] Does it sound like I have a point of view?
- [ ] Is there a specific human behind these words?
- [ ] Would this get a reaction (agreement, disagreement, laughter)?

## Sound Confident

- [ ] No hedging words (might, could, perhaps, arguably)
- [ ] Direct statements, not qualified ones
- [ ] Specific claims with numbers/examples
- [ ] No "I think" or "In my opinion" unless genuinely uncertain

## Sound Direct

- [ ] No setup language
- [ ] No summaries at the end
- [ ] No restating the question
- [ ] Get to the point in the first sentence

## Sound Specific

- [ ] Nouns, not abstractions
- [ ] Numbers, not "many" or "most"
- [ ] Examples, not generalizations
- [ ] "You" and "your", not "one" or "the user"

## Sound Human

- [ ] Occasional imperfection (short fragments, conversational asides)
- [ ] Varying sentence length
- [ ] No AI-sounding vocabulary
- [ ] Sounds like it was written by one person, not a committee
