# Writing Style — Before/After Examples

## Business Email

**❌ Before:**
> We are writing to inform you that we have implemented a robust new system that will foster greater synergy between our teams and streamline your workflow processes. This holistic solution leverages cutting-edge technology to create a paradigm shift in how we collaborate.

**✅ After:**
> We built a new system. It cuts your admin time in half. Here's how it works.

---

## LinkedIn Post

**❌ Before:**
> While many sales teams struggle with lead generation, we have discovered a proven methodology that not only increases pipeline velocity but also fosters stronger customer relationships. The key takeaway is that consistency matters more than volume.

**✅ After:**
> Most sales teams chase volume. We chased consistency. 6 months later: 3x more qualified leads, 40% faster close rate. The boring thing works.

---

## Proposal

**❌ Before:**
> Our comprehensive audit will delve into the landscape of your current revenue operations, identifying robust opportunities for optimization. We will provide actionable insights that foster sustainable growth.

**✅ After:**
> We'll find where your revenue leaks. We'll fix it. You'll see the difference in 30 days.

---

## Headline

**❌ Before:**
> Key Takeaways: Why Our Solution Matters for Your Business

**✅ After:**
> The €50K You're Leaving on the Table Every Quarter

---

## Bullet List

**❌ Before:**
> - We provide comprehensive support.
> - We provide fast response times.
> - We provide expert consultants.

**✅ After:**
> - 24/7 support. No voicemail.
> - 4-minute average response.
> - Consultants who've actually closed deals.

---

## Opening Sentence

**❌ Before:**
> In today's rapidly evolving business landscape, companies must leverage robust strategies to foster sustainable growth.

**✅ After:**
> Your competitors are already doing this. You're not.

---

## Call to Action

**❌ Before:**
> We would be delighted to discuss how our holistic approach can streamline your operations and foster greater success.

**✅ After:**
> Book a 15-minute call. I'll show you exactly where the leak is.
