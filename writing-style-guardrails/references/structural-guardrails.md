# Writing Style — Structural Guardrails

## Sentence Rhythm

Good writing breathes. Bad writing marches.

**Bad:**
> We believe in transparency. We believe in honesty. We believe in results.

**Good:**
> We believe in transparency. Honesty too. And yeah — results.

## Paragraph Rhythm

Alternate short punchy paragraphs with longer explanatory ones.

**Bad:**
> [4 sentences about feature A]
> [4 sentences about feature B]
> [4 sentences about feature C]

**Good:**
> [2 sentences about feature A]
>
> [1 sentence punchline about why A matters]
>
> [5 sentences about feature B, including a specific example]
>
> [3 sentences about feature C, ending with a question to the reader]

## List Structure

Vary the pattern within lists. Don't make every bullet a complete sentence.

**Bad:**
> - We provide 24/7 support.
> - We provide fast response times.
> - We provide expert consultants.

**Good:**
> - 24/7 support. No voicemail, no hold music.
> - Average response time: 4 minutes.
> - Consultants who've actually done the work, not just read about it.

## Heading Style

Avoid generic headings. Make them specific to the content.

**Bad:**
> ## Key Takeaways
> ## Why It Matters
> ## Final Thoughts

**Good:**
> ## The One Thing That Actually Changes Minds
> ## What This Costs If You Ignore It
> ## Do This Today
