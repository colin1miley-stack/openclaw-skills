# Systems Care — Renewal Guide

## Month 10: Plant Seeds

- "As we approach month 12, I wanted to check — what's changed in your business?"
- "Based on what we've learned, I'd recommend [X] for year 2."

## Month 11: Propose Renewal

- "Our retainer renews next month. Based on [specific results], I recommend [plan]."
- Offer annual discount: €12,500/year (save €2,500 vs. monthly)

## Month 12: Close or Transition

- If renewing: celebrate, set year 2 goals
- If not: professional handoff, documentation transfer, keep door open
