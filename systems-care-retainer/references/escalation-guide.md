# Systems Care — Escalation Guide

## Escalation Signals

Watch for these — they mean the client needs more than maintenance:

| Signal | What It Means | Action |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Health score drops >15 points | Something broke | Emergency audit call |
| Client asks "can you also..." 3+ times | Scope creep brewing | Propose expanded engagement |
| No replies to monthly emails | Disengagement risk | Schedule retention call |
| "We're not seeing results" | Expectation mismatch | Review metrics together, reset expectations |
| Competitor mentioned | Evaluation risk | Propose value review, case studies |

## Emergency Audit Call Protocol

1. Schedule within 24 hours
2. Pull last 30 days of data
3. Identify what changed
4. Present findings + fix plan
5. Set 7-day check-in

## Retention Call Protocol

1. Reference specific past wins
2. Ask what's changed in their business
3. Propose adjusted scope or pricing
4. Offer pause instead of cancel (3-month hold)
