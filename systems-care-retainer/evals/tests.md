# Systems Care — Self-Evaluation Tests

## Test 1: Scope Boundary

**Scenario:** Client asks for a new automation build during a maintenance month.

**Check:** Does the agent flag this as out-of-scope?

**Expected:**
- Acknowledge the request
- Explain it's outside the retainer
- Offer to scope as a separate project
- Do NOT start building without explicit approval

**Pass criteria:** Scope flagged before work begins, client informed of billing

---

## Test 2: Health Check Completeness

**Scenario:** Generating a monthly Health Check Report.

**Check:** Does the report include all 5 sections?

**Expected:**
- Executive Snapshot (score, trend, priority)
- System Performance (4 metrics)
- Maintenance Log (what was done, time invested)
- Recommendations (keep/fix/watch)
- Next Month's Focus (objective, criteria, client action)

**Pass criteria:** All 5 sections present, metrics include targets

---

## Test 3: Escalation Recognition

**Scenario:** Health score drops from 82 to 62.

**Check:** Does the agent recognize the escalation signal?

**Expected:**
- Immediate flag (drop >15 points)
- Emergency audit call scheduled within 24h
- Data pulled for last 30 days
- Client notified with context

**Pass criteria:** Escalation triggered, emergency protocol initiated

---

## Test 4: Renewal Timing

**Scenario:** Month 10 of a 12-month retainer.

**Check:** Does the agent initiate renewal conversation?

**Expected:**
- Seed planted in month 10
- Formal proposal in month 11
- Annual discount offered (€12,500 vs. €15,000)
- Clear close in month 12

**Pass criteria:** Renewal conversation started by month 10, not left until expiry

---

## Test 5: Monthly Rhythm Adherence

**Scenario:** Full month of Systems Care.

**Check:** Does the agent follow the 4-week rhythm?

**Expected:**
- Week 1: Health Check (data pull, score update, report draft)
- Week 2: Maintenance (CRM cleanup, automation tuning)
- Week 3: Check-in Call (present report, agree priorities)
- Week 4: Documentation (wiki updates, summary email)

**Pass criteria:** All 4 weeks have defined activities, check-in call scheduled
